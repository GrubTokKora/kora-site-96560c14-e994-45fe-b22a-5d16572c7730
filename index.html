Our Story

Authentic Indian flavors
in Quakertown

Green Chili Indian Restaurant brings the bold, layered flavors of traditional Indian cooking to the heart of Quakertown, PA. From the first bite of our tandoori to the last spoonful of our slow-simmered curries, every dish is crafted with care using time-honored recipes and freshly ground spices.

"Spice-forward, traditional, and made fresh — because great Indian food deserves nothing less."

We specialize in takeout, so every order is prepared to travel beautifully — arriving at your table just as vibrant and aromatic as it left our kitchen. Whether you're craving buttery Chicken Makhani, a fragrant Biryani, or crisp golden Samosas, we have something to satisfy every appetite.
